# Equipment Sell Calculator

The **Equipment Sell Calculator** provides a floating, interactive tool directly on the Equipment Market to help you precisely calculate optimal listing prices for your items. Inspired by Lebly, it ensures you can quickly determine exactly how much a buyer needs to pay for you to receive your target profit after taxes.

![Equipment Sell Calculator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/beertierchen/warera-prost/main/docs/wiki/images/equip-sell-calc.png)

## Key Features

- **Precision Rounding**: Emulates the game's exact 3-decimal rounding logic to calculate the absolute most accurate listing prices.
- **Dynamic Tax Calculation**: Allows you to adjust the market tax percentage on the fly to see how it affects the final required buyer-pays amount.
- **Click to Copy**: Displays a large result and intermediate calculation ticks. Clicking on any number instantly copies it to your clipboard so you can paste it directly into the game's sell window.
- **Interactive Ticks**: Shows variations around your target price (`-0.002` to `+0.001` increments) allowing you to fine-tune your listing price for psychological pricing or exact profit margins.

## How It Works

1. **Enter Your Target**: Input the net gold amount you want to receive *after* taxes in the calculator.
2. **Set the Tax**: Adjust the tax percentage (defaults to your last used or standard market tax).
3. **Pick Your Price**: The calculator generates the exact listing price required, alongside a list of tick adjustments. Click your preferred value to copy it, then paste it in the game's listing box.

## Configuration

This feature can be toggled on or off in the **Settings & Cheat Sheet** panel under the "Market" category.
